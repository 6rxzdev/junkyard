Scrap collection

Every big build starts with what you find in the yard.

Where do scraps come from?

The central junkyard is the main scavenging area. Explore the middle of the map, collect available scrap materials, then carry them back to your personal base. Keep an eye out for different scrap types, since some may be needed for specific compressor recipes.

Collector's tip: Before using the compressor, save the specific scrap types required for a recipe. Avoid using up materials you need for a valuable block.

The Trash Compressor

Make more value from the right combination of materials.

⚙️
MATERIAL PROCESSING

From loose scraps to solid blocks

The Trash Compressor transforms specific combinations of scraps into a compressed block. Once created, take the block to your base and place it like other income-generating items. Compressed blocks are designed to earn more money than the scraps used to make them.

Remember: The required scrap combination and exact earnings depend on the recipe configured in the game.

Your base & earnings

Turn every open space into a source of income.

How passive income works

Place scraps and compressed blocks inside your own base to earn money automatically every 1 second. The more valuable your placed items are, the more you can build toward a stronger income setup. Make room for new items and prioritize compressed blocks when you have the right materials.

💡
Build smarter, not just bigger.

Collect materials consistently, learn which scraps work together, and upgrade your base with higher-value compressed blocks as you progress.

Tips & tricks

Simple habits to help your junkyard grow.

📦

Keep materials organized

Track which scraps you have so you can prepare for compression recipes without wasting useful materials.

⏱️

Keep your base productive

Place collected items instead of leaving them unused. Your base is where your passive income starts.

📈

Think in upgrades

Use the compressor to turn the right scraps into blocks that can earn more than the original materials.
