Scrap collection

Every big build starts with what you find in the yard.

Where do scraps come from?

The central junkyard is the main scavenging area. Explore the middle of the map, collect available scrap materials, then carry them back to your personal base. Keep an eye out for different scrap types, since some may be needed for specific compressor recipes.

Collector's tip: Before using the compressor, save the specific scrap types required for a recipe. Avoid using up materials you need for a valuable block.

How does your base work?

Your personal base is where you place the items you collect. Items placed in your base can generate money over time. Organize your materials, make room for valuable blocks, and check your earnings as you progress.
